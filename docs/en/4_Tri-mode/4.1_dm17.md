## 3. Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Keycode | Function | Notes |
| ---------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hold NumberLock | LT(1, KC_NUM) | Tap: toggle NumberLock; hold: FN (combo key) | Indicator stays solid white while NumberLock is on |
| Hold `*` | LT(2, KC_PSLS) | Tap: `*`; hold: FN2 (triggers layer-2 combos) | The `*` indicator stays solid white while held |
| FN [+] 7 | RGB_TOG | Per-switch RGB on / off | Hold NumberLock and press 7 |
| FN [+] 0 | RGB_MOD | Cycle RGB effect | Hold NumberLock and press 0 |
| FN [+] 8 | RGB_SAI | Increase saturation | Hold NumberLock and press 8 |
| FN [+] 2 | RGB_SAD | Decrease saturation | Hold NumberLock and press 2 |
| FN [+] 4 | RGB_HUD | Increase hue | Hold NumberLock and press 4 |
| FN [+] 6 | RGB_HUI | Decrease hue | Hold NumberLock and press 6 |
| FN [+] - | RGB_VAI | Increase brightness | Hold NumberLock and press - |
| FN [+] + | RGB_VAD | Decrease brightness | Hold NumberLock and press + |
| `*` [+] - | NK_TOGG | Toggle NKRO / 6KRO | Hold NumberLock, then press `*` and - |
| `*` [+] NumberLock | QK_CLEAR_EEPROM | Factory reset | |
